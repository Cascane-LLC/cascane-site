Delete Your Account

You can delete your Cascane account and everything you created on it at any time. This page works without signing in or having the app installed.

In the app

If you have the app installed, the fastest way to delete your account is inside it: open Settings → Security → Delete account. This removes your account immediately.

From the web

If you don't have the app, submit a deletion request below and our support team will verify and complete it for you. Once verified, we permanently delete your account and the content you created within 30 days.

You can also email us directly at support@cascane.app with the email address on your account, and we'll take it from there.
