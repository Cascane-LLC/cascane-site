About

About Cascane

Cascane LLC is building a messaging platform that puts voice at the center of every conversation. We believe the best communication happens when you can hear someone's tone, emotion, and personality — not just read their words.

Our app features voice messages, group conversations called Convos, public Channels for communities, and ephemeral messaging that keeps conversations fresh and in the moment.

We're creating a space where people connect authentically, one voice message at a time.
